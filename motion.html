Research and academic projects

01 / COMPUTER VISION

Vision-based active protection system

I built a research prototype that uses YOLOv5 to detect objects and Raspberry Pi hardware to connect detection with sensing and servo control. The work led to a paper that received the Best Paper Award at ICBSII 2023.

02 / SPEECH & DEEP LEARNING

Speech emotion recognition

I compared deep learning models for classifying emotions in recorded speech, using wavelet decomposition and MFCC features. The project included experiments across four speech datasets and a Tkinter interface for microphone input.

03 / MATHEMATICAL MODELLING

Mathematical modelling of glass etching

For my MSc thesis at the University of Limerick, I replicated PDE and ODE models in MATLAB to study glass etching. I later explored a Python simulation to visualise changes in the surface over time.

04 / MEDICAL IMAGING

Kidney CT and biomarker classification

Large-scale data and network analysis
